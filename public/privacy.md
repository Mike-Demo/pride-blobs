# Privacy policy

Pridatar is designed to have almost nothing to say here.

## What we do not collect

- **No accounts, no logins, no personal data.** There is no sign-up and no
  profile; we have no database of users.
- **No cookies.** The site sets no cookies.
- **No uploads.** Seeds, names, and avatars are generated and rendered
  entirely in your browser. Nothing you type ever leaves your device or is
  sent to a server.
- **No sale or sharing of data.** There is nothing to sell or share.

## What we do measure

- **Anonymous pageview counts** via a self-hosted Umami analytics instance
  (umami-lite.view.fast). Umami is cookieless; it records that a page was
  visited, not who visited it.
- **Framework-level session storage**: the app framework stores scroll
  position in `sessionStorage` so back-navigation restores your place. It is
  cleared when the tab closes and never transmitted.

## Third parties

- **Google Fonts**: the site loads font files from Google Fonts to render
  text. Google receives standard HTTP request data (IP address, user agent)
  when fonts load, per Google's own privacy policy.
- **No advertising, no trackers, no social widgets.**
